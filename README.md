# **My Favorite Movie/Series**

> ### **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)**
> Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse is an animated superhero film based on the **Marvel Comics** character **Spider-Man**. The story focuses on Miles Morales who suddenly develops the powers of Spider-Man after getting bitten by a radioactive spider. Now with his newfound powers, Miles teams up with five spider people from other dimensions to take down the Kingpin.

> ### **KonoSuba**
> KonoSuba: God's Blessing on This Wonderful World is a Japanese **light novel** series written by **Natsume Akatsuki**. The series follows **Kazuma Satou**, a boy who is sent to a fantasy world following his death, where he forms a dysfunctional adventuring party with a useless goddess, an explosion-obsessed archmage, and a masochistic crusader.

> ### **Mobile Suit Gundam: Iron-Blooded Orphans**
> Mobile Suit Gundam: Iron-Blooded Orphans is a 2015 Japanese television mecha anime series and the fourteenth mainline entry in **Sunrise**'s long running **Gundam** franchise. Iron-Blooded Orphans follows the exploits of juvenile soldiers who establish their own security company after rebelling against the adults who betrayed.
